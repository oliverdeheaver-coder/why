Biathlon

Race hard, then calm down enough to hit five targets. Try the range yourself.

The sport

Biathlon mixes cross-country skiing with rifle shooting. You race round a loop, come into the range out of breath, and shoot at five targets. Each miss means skiing a 150 m penalty loop in a sprint race, so a calm heart matters as much as fast legs.
