Oliver's Activity Day

Three hours packed with things to make, build and win. Everyone works in teams, switches activity every half hour or so, and goes home with something they made.

The plan

  1. Karaoke to warm everyone up.
  2. Treasure hunt with a challenge at every clue.
  3. Build challenge straw rockets, spaghetti-and-marshmallow towers and marble runs.
  4. Pizza making for lunch. Everyone makes their own.
  5. Create a board game with cardboard, dice and pens.
  6. Coding course making games and gadgets with micro:bits.
